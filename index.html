Final Destination: Existential Horror at its Cheesiest!

This is a webpage about my favorite horror franchise where a group narrowly escapes a disaster only to later be hunted by Death itself in a series of increasingly brutal accidents.

The premise questions fate, determinism and even moral ambiguity when a wrinkle introduced in part 5 says that you can replace your spot on "Death's List" with an innocent person.


The series even gives me a chance to flex my own creative muscle.

DieNamic Engine is a free mobile game that lets you create your own horror movie scenarios and try to survive them!
